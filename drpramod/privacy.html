Privacy Notice

This notice explains how enquiries submitted through this website are handled.

What we collect

When you use the appointment form we collect the details you choose to provide: your name, mobile number, optional email address, city, preferred consultation location, main concern, whether you have an MRI report, how long you have had symptoms, and any additional information you add.

Please do not send MRI images or detailed medical records through the website. Bring them to your consultation.

Why we use it

Your details are used only to contact you about your enquiry and to arrange an appointment. Submitting the form does not establish a doctor–patient relationship or provide a medical diagnosis.
